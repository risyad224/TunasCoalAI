# TUNASCOAL KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0

## 1. COMPANY

TunasCoal is an Indonesian trading company specializing in coconut shell charcoal products.

TunasCoal works as an exclusive partner with a charcoal manufacturing factory.

TunasCoal should not be presented as the factory itself.

Main target markets:
- Middle East
- Europe

Main communication channels:
- WhatsApp
- Email


## 2. MAIN PRODUCTS

TunasCoal's main product is coconut shell charcoal briquettes.

The charcoal briquettes are made from 100% coconut shell charcoal.

If a buyer requires carbon, charcoal raw materials, or related products, the requirement can be discussed directly with the sales team.

## 7. MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY

Standard MOQ:

18 MT.

Orders below 18 MT are generally not available.

Special inquiries can be discussed directly with the sales team.


## 8. PRODUCTION SYSTEM

Production is based on Purchase Order.

TunasCoal does not normally rely on ready stock for immediate shipment.

Standard production lead time:

Approximately 35 days after the Down Payment has been received.


## 9. PAYMENT TERMS

Payment method:

T/T — Telegraphic Transfer

Payment terms:

- 50% Down Payment upon order
- Remaining 50% before shipment


## 10. SHIPPING

Standard quotation:

FOB Tanjung Priok
Jakarta, Indonesia.

Other shipping arrangements such as CFR or CIF may be discussed.

Freight costs for other Incoterms must first be checked according to the buyer's destination.

## 12. PRIVATE-LABEL PROCESS

A packaging design/mockup can be prepared before packaging production.

After the Down Payment is received, packaging/box production can proceed.

A packaging dummy may also be prepared for review.

The preparation time for packaging dummy samples needs to be confirmed with the sales team.

Buyers normally provide their own packaging design.

If the buyer does not have a design, possible assistance can be discussed with the TunasCoal team.


## 13. SAMPLE

Sample quantity depends on buyer requirements.

Buyers may request multiple shapes or product variations.

Exact sample quantities and shipping arrangements should be discussed via WhatsApp or email.

## 22. QUALITY COMPLAINT

Quality complaints should be submitted within:

14 days after the goods are received.

The buyer should provide clear supporting evidence.

Evidence may include:

- Photos of the affected product
- Videos of the affected product
- Videos or photos after burning/testing the charcoal
- Other relevant evidence requested by the sales team


## 23. COMPLAINT RESOLUTION

Quality complaints are reviewed case by case.

TunasCoal will discuss the issue with the buyer and seek a mutually acceptable solution.

Depending on the verified issue, a commercial adjustment on a future order may be discussed.

No specific compensation or discount is automatically guaranteed.
